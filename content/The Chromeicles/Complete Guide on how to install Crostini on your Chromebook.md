## What You Need To Know

Crostini is practically an add-on to Chrome OS, allowing you to further use your device by downloading apps, coding environments, and much more. It is a Debian-based system on your device, allowing you to access the capabilities of Linux on your device, rather than being confined to Chrome OS.

## How To Do It

1. Click the time on the bottom-right hand corner of your screen.
2. Click the gear icon, and then navigate to About ChromeOS
3. Click on Set Up next to Linux Development Environment
4. It will ask you for a username. Use the default one.
5. It will ask you for a storage amount. Use the default one for now.
6. After it is done processing, go to your apps screen and type in “Terminal” in the search bar. Click on it.
7. Click on “penguin”.
8. You should get a screen where you can type something in with no error signs. If you did, congrats! You have installed Crostini on your Chromebook!

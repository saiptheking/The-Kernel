> "We are all now connected by the internet, like neurons in a giant brain." Stephen Hawking

The internet is vast and diverse. We can do so many things on it, from watching movies to getting work done. But the foundation of internet is code. Let's dive in deeper.

## The Building Blocks of the Internet

The internet is made up of pages of HTML, Javascript, and Python, known as webpages. These webpages can be assigned a domain identifier, known as it's web domain or URL. This is essentially what the internet is: a bunch of HTML, Javascript, and Python with an identifier. 

## But How Do We Access It?

The internet is accessible through a server. A server is a device that can host, recieve, or send files and information to and from itself. These servers allow you to access the internet through a Wifi connection.

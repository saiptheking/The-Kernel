Note taking is a key skill, digital or on paper. Types of it include:

- Standard Outline: Used to emphasize the [[Productivity/Notetaking/Hierarchy of Ideas]].
- [[Cornell Notes_10222024172859 1.pdf\|Cornell Notes]]: Improves retention and understanding of the material
- [[Box and bullet notes_10222024191354 1.pdf\|Box and Bullet Notes]] Similar to standard outline, emphasis on central ideas in [[Productivity/Notetaking/Hierarchy of Ideas]]
- [[SQR3 Notes_10242024174426 1.pdf\|SQR3(Survey, Question, Read, Recite, and Review) Notes]]: Q/A based Note-taking, better for retention and understanding of material, and for immediate test studying

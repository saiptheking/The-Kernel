My Obsidian vault is fairly big. It is my workspace and storehouse for everything, whether it is school, personal, or anything in between.
![[Attachments/Pasted image 20260406210359.png]]

In my vault I store files such as PDFs, images, documents, and other things, along with notes. I typically use my vault for school, such as for studying for a test. Inside my main vault, I have the following folders:

- 00 Inbox
- 01 School
- 02 Projects
- 03 Creative Vault
- 04 Journal & Planning
- 05 Templates
- 06 Knowledge Vault
- 07 Calendar
- 08 Personal
- 09 Archive
- 10 Attachments
